    2027 · The decade that decides

    GAME OF AGI

    You chair a newly formed council that steers frontier AI policy for the US-aligned bloc. Over the next 13 years, systems will likely become smarter than any human. Every year you face a decision about racing China, releasing open weights, biodefense, neural interfaces and alignment.

    Each year the dice are rolled against three catastrophes: an engineered pandemic, loss of control, and great-power war. Reach the superintelligence threshold with alignment ready, or reach 2040 on a careful path.

    Some things nobody knows yet: whether AI turns out aligned by default, how hard alignment really is, and whether insiders would blow the whistle on a power grab. Each game draws them in secret. You find out at the end.

    This is a toy model built to make trade-offs tangible, not a forecast. Every probability comes from transparent, adjustable assumptions (see "How the model works"). Reasonable experts disagree about nearly all of them.

    YOUR DECISIONS

      Under the hood

      How the model works

      Game of AGI is a yearly Monte Carlo model of 2027–2040. Every equation and constant below is the one model.js runs. Tags show where each number comes from: AI 2040 is taken from or calibrated to AI 2040: Plan A, Bio decomposition is calibrated to a separate factored Monte Carlo estimate of bio risk, and Assumption is a judgment call in this model.

      1. One simulated year

      1. Compute this year's three catastrophe hazards from the current state (section 3).
      2. Draw one number u \sim U(0,1). If u < P_{\text{bio}}, a pandemic ends the future; else if u < P_{\text{bio}} + P_{\text{loc}}, loss of control; else if u < P_{\text{bio}} + P_{\text{loc}} + P_{\text{war}}, great-power war.
      3. Otherwise apply the yearly updates (section 5), clamp every state variable to 0–100 and move to the next year.
      4. If frontier capability reaches 100, resolve the superintelligence handoff; in 2040, resolve the ending (section 4).

      The Futures Lab runs 2,000 futures with the levers held fixed. In the campaign, each decision card also moves levers and state directly (events.js).

      3. Annual hazards

      Engineered pandemic (100M+ deaths):

      P_{\text{bio}} = 1 - e^{-k\,g}, \qquad g = \Big[\sigma\big(\tfrac{O-62}{6}\big) + 0.15\,\sigma\big(\tfrac{C-75}{6}\big)\big(1-\tfrac{A}{100}\big)\Big]\Big(1-\tfrac{B}{100}\Big)^{2}
      k = \frac{-\ln\left(1-R_{\text{bio}}\right)}{G_{\text{ref}}}, \qquad G_{\text{ref}} = \sum_{t=2027}^{2036} g_t^{\text{default path}} \approx 3.32

      The first term in g is uplift from open weights, which dominates once open models pass expert level; the second is a small leakage from closed models. The scale k makes the default path reproduce the drawn decade risk exactly, and policy moves risk through g. Bio decomposition The thresholds and the leakage weight are Assumption. The risk panel shows the expectation over R_{\text{bio}}, about 57 times the median because rare bad worlds dominate it.

      Loss of control:

      P_{\text{loc}} = (1-S)\cdot\min\Big(0.6,\ 0.3\,\sigma\big(\tfrac{C-\hat{A}-36}{6}\big)\,\sigma\big(\tfrac{C-78}{4}\big)\Big(0.4+0.9\,\tfrac{H}{100}\Big)\Big)
      \hat{A} = \begin{cases} A & C_{\text{US}} \ge C_{\text{CN}} \\ A\cdot\min\big(1,\ 0.55+0.45\,\tfrac{K}{100}+0.3\,\tau\big) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}

      Risk grows once capability passes top-expert level and outruns alignment. Hidden internal AI amplifies it, since AI 2040 argues most takeover risk comes from models run inside labs AI 2040. When control is lost, the future ends as a Benevolent Takeover if W = 1 and as Loss of Control otherwise. \hat{A} is effective alignment: if China leads, its lab uses US-bloc alignment work only as far as coordination and published research allow. Coefficients are Assumption.

      Great-power war:

      P_{\text{war}} = 0.055\Big(1-\tfrac{K}{100}\Big)^{2}(0.3+0.7\,q)\,\sigma\big(\tfrac{C-60}{8}\big), \qquad q = \operatorname{clamp}\Big(1-\tfrac{|C_{\text{US}}-C_{\text{CN}}|}{25}\Big)

      A close race, low trust and high strategic stakes raise it. Assumption

      4. Endings

      Superintelligence handoff. When C reaches 100:

      \Pr(\text{handoff goes well}) = \begin{cases} 1 & S = 1 \\ \sigma\big(\tfrac{\hat{A}-\beta}{6}\big) & S = 0 \end{cases}, \qquad \beta = 45 + 32\,\delta + 0.2\,(H-30) - 10\,\tfrac{N}{100}

      The bar \beta rises with hidden difficulty and with hidden internal AI, and falls when BCIs let humans check AI reasoning directly. A failed handoff is loss of control, which ends as a Benevolent Takeover if W = 1. A successful one ends in lock-in with probability

      P_{\text{lock}} = \sigma\big(\tfrac{P-64}{6}\big)\,(1-0.6\,v)\,(1-\omega)\,(1-0.6\,\rho), \qquad \rho = \max\Big(\operatorname{clamp}\big(1-\tfrac{|C_{\text{US}}-C_{\text{CN}}|}{15}\big),\ \operatorname{clamp}\big(\tfrac{O-80}{20}\big),\ 0.85\,\tau\Big)

      and otherwise in an Aligned Transition. Plurality \rho is high when a rival can replicate AGI before takeoff completes, open weights are near the frontier, or research is public so many labs can follow (the last from AI 2040). In 2040, a future that has not ended becomes Locked-In Power if T < 45 and a lock-in roll succeeds, The Careful Path if A \ge C and K > 55, and Muddling Through otherwise.

      5. Yearly updates

      Capability:

      \Delta C_{\text{US}} = 7\,\kappa(r)\,\varphi\,(1-s-0.08\,v)\,U(C_{\text{US}}) + \varepsilon
      \Delta C_{\text{CN}} = 7\,\kappa(r_{\text{CN}})\,\varphi\,m\,U(C_{\text{CN}}) + \varepsilon + 0.25\,\tau\,\max(0,\ C_{\text{US}}-C_{\text{CN}})
      U(c) = \begin{cases} 1 & c < 50 \\ 3^{(c-50)/20} & 50 \le c < 70 \\ \min\big(40,\ 3\,(40/3)^{(c-70)/20}\big) & c \ge 70 \end{cases} \qquad \kappa(r) = \Big(\frac{\max(0,\ r-0.03)}{0.52}\Big)^{0.74}
      r_{\text{CN}} = \min\Big(1,\ \max\Big(0.05,\ 0.6 + 0.45\big(r(1-0.5\,s)-0.5\big) - 0.55\,\tfrac{K}{100}\Big)\Big)

      U is the AI R&D speed-up from AI labor: 3× at Automated Coder and about 40× near top-expert-dominating AI AI 2040. The compute exponent 0.74 encodes "10× less compute makes the intelligence explosion 5.5× slower" AI 2040, and the base rate 7 puts the default path at Automated Coder around 2030 and superintelligence about a year later AI 2040. Safety spending and oversight take compute away from capabilities. China's race intensity responds to the US posture and falls with coordination; publishing research helps China close the gap Assumption. Here \varepsilon \sim U(-1,1), m is the export-control multiplier set by campaign choices (1 in the Lab), and each year a surprise breakthrough adds 3–8 points to the US and 2–6 to China with probability 0.15.

      The deal. A verified US–China deal is in force when K \ge 60 and d \ge 0.5 and no deal has collapsed. It holds both blocs at 78, top-human-expert level, like Plan A's 2035 pause. Each year it collapses with probability 0.06, about 46% over ten years against AI 2040's roughly 48%; a collapse cuts K by 40 and the deal never returns AI 2040.

      Alignment:

      \Delta A = \frac{0.55\,\big(1.9 + 10\,e(s) + 1.5\,v\big)\,\big(1 + (U(C_{\text{US}})-1)\,\ell(s)\big)\,\big(1 + 0.35\,\tfrac{K}{100} + 0.4\,\tau\big)\,\big(1 + 0.5\,\tfrac{N}{100}\big)}{\delta} + \varepsilon_A
      e(s) = \begin{cases} \frac{s-0.01}{0.29} & s \le 0.3 \\ 1 + 0.6\,\frac{s-0.3}{0.7} & s > 0.3 \end{cases} \qquad \ell(s) = 0.08 + 0.35\,\min\big(1,\ e(s)\big) + 0.5\,\frac{\max(0,\ s-0.3)}{0.7}

      The factors are: research effort (safety share, with diminishing returns past 30%, plus oversight); AI labor pointed at safety, so alignment also speeds up during takeoff, using the US bloc's own AI; shared international and public research; augmented researchers through BCIs; and the hidden difficulty \delta. Here \varepsilon_A \sim U(-0.5,0.5), and with probability 0.12 a year an alignment insight adds 3 to 12 points (more with a higher safety share). The 0.55 scale and the spread of \delta are set so fixed-lever versions of AI 2040's plans reproduce its p(alignment) estimates AI 2040; the other coefficients are Assumption.

      Everything else:

      \Delta H = 4\,r\,\sqrt{U(C)} - 9\,\tau - 5\,v - 6\,\tfrac{N}{100} - 0.5
      \Delta K = 13\,d + 2\,v + 3\,\tau - 6\,r - 0.5 + \varepsilon_K, \qquad \varepsilon_K \sim U(-1.5,\ 1.5)
      O \leftarrow O + 0.45\,\big(C\,(0.62 + 0.36\,o + 0.06\,\tau) - O\big)
      \Delta B = 9\,b + 1.2\,o - 1.6
      \Delta N = \min\big(20,\ (11\,n + 0.6)\,(1 + 0.4\,n\,(U(C)-1))\big)
      \Delta P = 10\,r\,(1 - 0.5\,o - 0.5\,\tau) + 0.06\,\max(0,\ C-60) - 2.5\,o - 4\,\tau + 2.2\,n\,(1-d) - 4\,v - 0.8
      \Delta T = -0.04\,\max(0,\ C-55) + 2.5\,\min\big(1,\ e(s)\big) + 1.5\,v + 1.5\,\tau - 1.5\,r\,\tfrac{P}{100} + 0.3

      Racing grows hidden internal AI, faster once AI automates R&D; transparency, inspections and augmented overseers pull it into view. Open-weights capability chases a policy-set share of the frontier. Biodefense decays without investment, and open tools help it a little. AI labor speeds BCIs up, but surgery, approval and rollout cap adoption at 20 points a year. Racing on closed models concentrates power; openness, transparency and oversight spread it. All coefficients in this block are Assumption.

      7. What it leaves out

      Covert projects and cheating on the deal (beyond a flat collapse rate), cyberattacks, countries other than the two blocs, misuse beyond bio, conflict between AGIs, anything after 2040, plans that change over time in the Futures Lab, and whether capability can really be put on one axis at all. Treat it as an argument you can poke at, not an oracle.
